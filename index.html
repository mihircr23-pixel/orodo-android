Privacy Policy for Orodo

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Orodo ("we", "our", or "the app") is a productivity application designed around the Reverse Pomodoro method. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and shared when you use Orodo.

1. Information We Collect

Orodo may process information you provide directly, including your name or display name, email address, account information, app preferences, study history, session information, efficiency data, progress information, and other data required to provide the app's features.

If you create an Orodo account, your email address is used for account authentication and related account functionality.

2. Local Data

Some Orodo information may be stored locally on your device. This can include preferences, timer information, study history, theme settings, and other app state required for the application to function.

3. Accounts and Cloud Synchronization

Orodo provides optional account and cloud synchronization features. When these features are used, information such as your account details, preferences, study history, progress, and app state may be transmitted to and stored using our cloud service provider, Supabase.

Cloud synchronization allows supported Orodo data to be restored and synchronized across devices.

4. Advertising

Orodo uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may collect or process information such as device information, advertising identifiers, IP address, interactions with advertisements, diagnostic information, and other information necessary to provide, measure, secure, and improve advertising services.

Depending on your location, applicable law, and your privacy choices, advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized.

5. Consent and Privacy Choices

Where required by applicable law, Orodo may request your consent before certain advertising-related data is processed. Google consent-management technology may be used to present privacy choices and manage advertising consent.

Available privacy choices may vary depending on your country or region.

6. How We Use Information

Information processed through Orodo may be used to:

  • Provide and maintain the application's features.
  • Create and authenticate user accounts.
  • Synchronize supported information between devices.
  • Restore user information after signing in.
  • Maintain study history, progress, preferences, and app state.
  • Display and measure advertisements.
  • Maintain security, prevent abuse, and diagnose technical problems.
  • Improve the reliability and functionality of Orodo.

7. Third-Party Services

Orodo uses third-party services that may process information according to their own privacy policies. These services include:

  • Supabase — account authentication, database services, and cloud synchronization.
  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads — advertising, ad measurement, fraud prevention, and related advertising services.

You can learn more about Google's privacy practices at Google Privacy Policy .

You can learn more about Supabase's privacy practices at Supabase Privacy Policy .

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers where necessary to operate Orodo, including providing authentication, cloud synchronization, advertising, security, and related technical services.

Information may also be disclosed where required by law or where reasonably necessary to protect users, prevent fraud or abuse, or protect legal rights.

9. Data Retention

Locally stored information may remain on your device until it is cleared, the app is uninstalled, or the relevant information is otherwise removed. Cloud information associated with an Orodo account may be retained while the account is active or as reasonably necessary to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, and enforce app policies.

10. Account and Data Deletion

Users who create an Orodo account can request deletion of their account and associated cloud data using the account deletion functionality provided within Orodo.

When an account deletion request is successfully processed, the Orodo account and associated Orodo cloud data are deleted, subject to information that may need to be retained where required by law, security requirements, fraud prevention, or other legitimate legal obligations.

11. Security

We use reasonable technical measures and third-party infrastructure designed to protect information processed through Orodo. However, no electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. Children's Privacy

Orodo is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe that personal information relating to a child has been provided improperly, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

13. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding your personal information, such as the right to request access, correction, deletion, or certain restrictions on processing, as provided by applicable law.

Advertising consent and privacy options may also be available through Google's consent-management interface where applicable.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Orodo's functionality, third-party services, or legal requirements change. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will indicate when the policy was most recently revised.

15. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or privacy-related requests, you can contact the Orodo developer through the official contact information provided on Orodo's Google Play Store listing.
