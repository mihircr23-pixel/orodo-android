Privacy Policy for Orodo

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Orodo – Reverse Pomodoro is a productivity and study timer application designed to help users track study sessions, breaks, study history, efficiency, and progress.

Information Collection

The current version of Orodo does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal user data with the developer or third parties.

Information such as study sessions, study history, preferences, theme settings, and personalization information is stored locally on the user's device.

Account & Sync

Orodo currently includes an Account & Sync interface, but cloud account synchronization is not enabled in the current release. Users do not need to create an account or sign in to use Orodo.

Advertising

Orodo does not currently display third-party advertisements.

Data Sharing

Orodo does not currently sell or share user data with third parties.

Data Security

Study information and application preferences used by the current version are stored locally on the user's device. Users should be aware that removing the application or clearing its application data may remove locally stored information.

Children's Privacy

Orodo is a general productivity and study application and is not specifically directed toward young children. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Orodo introduces new features or changes its data practices. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
