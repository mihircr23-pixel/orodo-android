Delete your Orodo account

Orodo – Reverse Pomodoro

You can request permanent deletion of your Orodo account and the data associated with it.

How to request account deletion

Send an account deletion request from the email address associated with your Orodo account to:

mihircr23@gmail.com

Use the subject Orodo Account Deletion Request.

Please do not include your password or other sensitive authentication information in the email.

What will be deleted?

When your deletion request is completed, your Orodo account and associated cloud-synced app data will be permanently deleted. This may include your study history, progress, preferences, profile information and other data associated with your account.

Data retention

Data may be retained where required for legal, security, fraud-prevention or regulatory purposes. Any such retained data will only be kept for the period required for those purposes.

Need help?

For questions about account deletion or privacy, contact mihircr23@gmail.com .
